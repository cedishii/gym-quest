# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-14)

- **Music keeps playing in the lobby.** Sound effects used to take Android's
  audio focus, which paused the music after the first button sound. Effects now
  mix with the music, music comes back if it was stopped, and it pauses while
  the app is in the background.
- **Attack effects:** explosions, smoke bursts, sparks and shockwaves for every
  attack, colored by element. Ultimates get a full explosion animation and a
  screen flash.
- **Livelier pets:** shading, rim light and an outline give the art depth. Pets
  breathe, sway, hop now and then, cast a soft shadow, and glow with drifting
  sparks of their element. The Fire pet now grins instead of frowning.
- **New app logo** on the home screen icon and the welcome screen.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-14)

First release.

- **Fitness:** profile with BMI, BMR and TDEE estimates, plus a safe calorie and
  macro target. A food log with 7,000+ offline USDA foods, Open Food Facts search
  and barcode lookup, and custom foods. Weekly workout plans built from your goal,
  experience, equipment and schedule. Workout logging with exercise photos,
  personal records and calorie estimates.
- **Pet RPG:** five elemental pets that grow and gain auras as they level.
  Workouts earn food to feed them, and five equipment slots change their stats.
- **Adventure:** a 20-stage map with five gym leaders and turn-based battles
  where element matchups matter, plus an Endless Tower after the champion.
- **Progression:** workout streaks (rest days allowed), streak milestones,
  17 achievements, and battle history.
- Everything is stored on the device. No account needed.
